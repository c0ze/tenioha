A LANGUAGE IN THE MAKING

          

            Japanese structure.
Functional foundations.
          

        

        

          

            Tenioha explores what happens when the little words in Japanese
            become checked parts of a program. It brings together immutable
            bindings, explicit types, lexical closures, algebraic data, and
            pattern matching.
          

          

            It’s a controlled programming language, with explicit word
            boundaries and declared spellings. The aim is to make argument roles
            visible and worth thinking about.
          

          

            Inspired by Kip, with
            Nadesiko as important Japanese
            prior art. Experimental, actively evolving, and open to curious
            readers.
